# 04 · Tricky Moments 难场面

## Unit 1: 没听懂怎么办 Asking to Repeat

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sorry, could you say that again? | 抱歉，你能再说一次吗？ | sorry, / could you say that aGAIN? |
| 2 | Could you speak slower, please? | 你能说慢一点吗？ | could you speak SLOWer, / please? |
| 3 | Do you mean this one? | 你是说这个吗？ | do you mean THIS one? |
| 4 | One moment, let me check. | 稍等，我看一下。 | ONE moment, / let me check. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| So, um, I was actually hoping to get the gel manicure today, but if the wait is really long I might just come back tomorrow instead, is that okay? | 大意：她想做光疗美甲，但如果等太久可能会改明天再来。听不懂就用求救句，听懂大意即胜利！ |
| Sorry, I know I'm early, but my friend said you guys might be able to squeeze me in for a quick eyebrow thing before my facial appointment, is there any chance? | 大意：她来早了，朋友说也许能在她的面部护理前先加做个眉毛。听不懂就用求救句，听懂大意即胜利！ |
| Actually, can we change it from the classic lashes to the volume ones instead? I saw a picture online and I think I'd like those better, if that's not too much trouble. | 大意：她想把嫁接睫毛从单根款换成浓密款。听不懂就用求救句，听懂大意即胜利！ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer walks in and speaks quickly.
>
> Customer: So, um, I was actually hoping to get the gel manicure today, but if the wait is really long I might just come back tomorrow instead, is that okay?
> You: Sorry, could you say that again?
> Customer: I'd like a manicure.
> You: Could you speak slower, please?
> Customer: Gel, please.
> You: Do you mean this one?
> Customer: Yes, it's gel.
> You: One moment, let me check.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "again" 重音在第二个音节：aGAIN，元音接近中文"给"，不要把重音错放在 a 上
- "could you" 常连读成 "COULD-juh"：跟前面学过的 "get your"（getcher）是同样的连读规律，不用一个字一个字分开读
- "moment" 重音在第一个音节：MOment，后面的 ment 要轻快带过

## Unit 2: 客户不满与补救 Service Recovery

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I'm so sorry about that. | 真的很抱歉。 | i'm so SORry about that. |
| 2 | Let me fix it for you. | 我马上帮你处理。 | let me FIX it for you. |
| 3 | No charge for this. | 这个不收费。 | no CHARGE for this. |
| 4 | Thank you for your patience. | 谢谢你的耐心等待。 | thank you for your PAtience. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| This isn't what I asked for. | 这不是我要的效果。 |
| One nail already chipped. | 有一个指甲已经掉漆了。 |
| It's okay, thanks for fixing it. | 没关系，谢谢你帮我处理。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer looks unhappy with her nails.
>
> Customer: This isn't what I asked for.
> You: I'm so sorry about that.
> Customer: One nail already chipped.
> You: Let me fix it for you.
> …(fixes the chipped nail)…
> You: No charge for this.
> Customer: It's okay, thanks for fixing it.
> You: Thank you for your patience.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "patience" 中的 ti 组合发 "sh" 音：整个词读起来像 "PAY-shuns"，不要按字面拼读
- "charge" 开头的 ch 是清晰的 "chuh" 音，跟 "chipped" 开头的 ch 是同一个音
- "chipped" 结尾的 -ed 因为前面是清辅音 p，要读成清音 t，听起来像 "chipt"，不要多读出一个音节

## Unit 3: 改约与取消 Rescheduling

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No problem, we can reschedule. | 没问题，我们可以帮你改约。 | no problem, / we can reSCHEdule. |
| 2 | What day works better? | 哪天对你更方便？ | what DAY works better? |
| 3 | I'll move you to Friday. | 我帮你改到周五。 | i'll move you to FRIday. |
| 4 | See you then! | 到时候见！ | see you THEN! |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I need to cancel tomorrow. | 我需要取消明天的预约。 |
| Can I move my appointment? | 我可以改一下预约时间吗？ |
| Friday at two works. | 周五两点可以。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer calls to change her appointment.
>
> Customer: I need to cancel tomorrow.
> You: No problem, we can reschedule.
> Customer: Can I move my appointment?
> You: What day works better?
> Customer: Friday at two works.
> You: I'll move you to Friday.
> …(books the new time)…
> You: See you then!

**Pronunciation notes**

- "reschedule" 重音在第二个音节：reSCHEdule，schedule 部分读作 "SKED-jul"，sch 组合读成 s 和 k 连在一起的音，不是"吸"
- "Friday" 重音在第一个音节：FRIday，跟 "Monday"、"Sunday" 这些星期词是同样的重音规律
- "cancel" 重音在第一个音节：CANcel，后面的 -cel 要轻快带过，不要把两个音节读得一样重

## Unit 4: 推荐产品与续卡 Upselling (soft)

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | This cream is great for dry skin. | 这款面霜很适合干性皮肤。 | this cream is great for DRY skin. |
| 2 | We have a package deal. | 我们有套餐优惠。 | we have a PACKage deal. |
| 3 | Five visits for the price of four. | 做五次只收四次的钱。 | FIVE visits for the price of FOUR. |
| 4 | No pressure, just letting you know! | 没有压力，就是跟你说一声！ | no PRESSure, / just letting you know! |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| How much is the package? | 套餐多少钱？ |
| I'll think about it. | 我再考虑一下。 |
| Sure, I'll take one. | 好，那我要一个。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer is finishing her facial.
>
> You: This cream is great for dry skin.
> Customer: How much is the package?
> You: We have a package deal.
> Customer: I'll think about it.
> You: Five visits for the price of four.
> …(hands her a flyer)…
> You: No pressure, just letting you know!
> Customer: Sure, I'll take one.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "package" 重音在第一个音节：PACKage，后面的 -age 读轻音"以之"，不要读成"阿举"
- "pressure" 重音在第一个音节：PRESSure，中间 ssu 读成"许"的音
- "visits" 重音在第一个音节：VIsits，后面的 -its 要轻快带过，不要把两个音节读得一样重
