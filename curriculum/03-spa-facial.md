# 03 · Spa & Facial 美容与皮肤管理

## Unit 1: 皮肤咨询 Skin Consult

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How is your skin feeling today? | 你今天皮肤感觉怎么样？ | how is your skin FEELing today? |
| 2 | Is your skin dry, oily, or combination? | 你的皮肤是干性、油性，还是混合性？ | is your skin DRY, OIly, or combiNAtion? |
| 3 | Any breakouts lately? | 最近有长痘痘吗？ | any BREAKouts lately? |
| 4 | Let me take a look. | 我来看一下。 | let me take a LOOK. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| My skin is really dry. | 我的皮肤很干。 |
| I've been breaking out. | 我最近一直在长痘。 |
| It gets oily in the afternoon. | 一到下午就出油。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer is seated for a skin consultation.
>
> You: Welcome in!
> Customer: Hi there!
> You: How is your skin feeling today?
> Customer: My skin is really dry.
> You: Is your skin dry, oily, or combination?
> Customer: It gets oily in the afternoon.
> You: Any breakouts lately?
> Customer: I've been breaking out.
> You: Let me take a look.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "oily" 中的 oi 双元音：整个读成一个滑动的音，从"哦"滑到"衣"，不要分成两个音节读
- "combination" 重音在第三个音节：combiNAtion，中文母语者容易把重音放在最后一个音节 tion 上，要注意重音落在 NA 上
- "breakouts" 是复合词，重音在前：BREAKouts，后面的 outs 要轻快带过

## Unit 2: 项目介绍与推荐 Recommending

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I recommend a hydrating facial. | 我推荐补水面部护理。 | i recomMEND a hydrating facial. |
| 2 | It's good for dry skin. | 这个对干性皮肤很好。 | it's good for DRY skin. |
| 3 | This one takes sixty minutes. | 这个项目需要六十分钟。 | this one takes SIXty minutes. |
| 4 | It includes a face massage. | 包含面部按摩。 | it includes a face masSAGE. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| What does it include? | 都包含什么？ |
| Which one is best for me? | 哪一个最适合我？ |
| How much is that one? | 那个多少钱？ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Talking about facial treatment options.
>
> You: I recommend a hydrating facial.
> Customer: What does it include?
> You: It includes a face massage.
> Customer: Which one is best for me?
> You: It's good for dry skin.
> Customer: How much is that one?
> You: That's fifty in total.
> Customer: How long does it take?
> You: This one takes sixty minutes.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "recommend" 重音在最后一个音节：recomMEND，前面的 rec-om 要读得又轻又快，不要把重音放在开头
- "massage" 重音在第二个音节：masSAGE，跟前面学过的 "remove"、"relax" 是同样的规律（重音在后半部分）
- "sixty" 重音在前：SIXty，跟前面学过的 thirty、fifty、twenty 是同样的规律

## Unit 3: 过敏与禁忌确认 Allergies & Safety

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do you have any allergies? | 你有什么过敏吗？ | do you have any ALlergies? |
| 2 | Is your skin sensitive? | 你的皮肤敏感吗？ | is your skin SENsitive? |
| 3 | Let me know if it stings. | 如果有刺痛感，请告诉我。 | let me know if it STINGS. |
| 4 | We'll do a patch test first. | 我们先做一个皮肤测试。 | we'll do a PATCH test first. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I'm allergic to nuts. | 我对坚果过敏。 |
| My skin is quite sensitive. | 我的皮肤比较敏感。 |
| It stings a little. | 有一点刺痛。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Checking for allergies before the treatment.
>
> You: Do you have any allergies?
> Customer: I'm allergic to nuts.
> You: Is your skin sensitive?
> Customer: My skin is quite sensitive.
> You: We'll do a patch test first.
> …(applies a small patch on the arm)…
> You: Let me know if it stings.
> Customer: It stings a little.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "allergies" 重音在第一个音节：ALlergies，后面音节要轻快带过
- "sensitive" 重音在第一个音节：SENsitive，中文母语者容易把每个音节读得一样重，要注意后两个音节轻一点
- "stings" 开头的 st 要连读：s 和 t 紧挨着读，中间不要加"额"的音，不然会多出一个音节

## Unit 4: 护理过程关怀 During Treatment

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is the pressure okay? | 力度可以吗？ | is the PRESSure okay? |
| 2 | Is the water too hot? | 水会太烫吗？ | is the water too HOT? |
| 3 | This might feel a little cold. | 这个可能会有点凉。 | this might feel a little COLD. |
| 4 | Just relax. | 放轻松就好。 | just reLAX. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| A bit lighter, please. | 轻一点，谢谢。 |
| That feels great. | 这样感觉很棒。 |
| A little warmer, please. | 再暖一点，谢谢。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Massaging the face during the treatment.
>
> You: Is the pressure okay?
> Customer: A bit lighter, please.
> You: Just relax.
> Customer: That feels great.
> You: Is the water too hot?
> Customer: A little warmer, please.
> You: This might feel a little cold.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "pressure" 重音在第一个音节：PRESSure，中间的 ssu 读成"许"的音，不是简单的 s
- "cold" 要读出圆唇的"欧"音，结尾的 d 轻轻收一下就好，不要吞掉
- "little" 中间的双 t 在口语里读成很轻很快的弹舌音，听起来接近一个轻巧的"d"，不要读成清晰用力的 t

## Unit 5: 售后建议 Aftercare

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drink plenty of water. | 多喝水。 | drink PLENty of water. |
| 2 | Avoid the sun today. | 今天避免晒太阳。 | avoid the SUN today. |
| 3 | Use sunscreen every day. | 每天都要用防晒。 | use SUNscreen every day. |
| 4 | A little redness is normal. | 有一点泛红是正常的。 | a little redness is NORmal. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| When can I wear makeup? | 我什么时候可以化妆？ |
| What products should I use? | 我应该用什么产品？ |
| Good to know! | 好的，知道了！ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Finishing up the facial.
>
> You: A little redness is normal.
> Customer: Good to know!
> You: Drink plenty of water. Avoid the sun today.
> Customer: When can I wear makeup?
> You: Use sunscreen every day.
> Customer: What products should I use?

**Pronunciation notes**

- "plenty" 重音在第一个音节：PLENty，跟前面学过的 thirty、twenty 是同样的重音规律
- "sunscreen" 是复合词，重音在前：SUNscreen，后面的 screen 要轻快带过
- "normal" 重音在第一个音节：NORmal，结尾的 l 要用舌尖轻轻抵住上门牙后面的牙龈收尾，不要卷舌
