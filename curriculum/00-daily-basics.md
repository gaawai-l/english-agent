# 00 · Daily Basics 日常基础

> Curriculum format: every unit has Core sentences / Customer lines /
> Mini dialogue / Pronunciation notes. The agent teaches ONE unit per
> session by default and never dumps the whole file to the learner.

## Unit 1: 问候与告别 Greetings & Goodbyes

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hi! Welcome! | 你好！欢迎光临！ | HI! / WELcome! |
| 2 | How are you? | 你今天好吗？ | how ARE you? |
| 3 | I'm good, thanks! | 我很好，谢谢！ | i'm GOOD, / THANKS! |
| 4 | Have a good day! | 祝你今天愉快！（送客用） | have a GOOD day! |
| 5 | See you next time! | 下次见！ | see you NEXT time! |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Hi there! | 你好呀！ |
| I'm good, how about you? | 我挺好的，你呢？ |
| Thanks, you too! | 谢谢，你也是！ |
| Bye! | 拜拜！ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer walks in.
>
> You: Hi! Welcome!
> Customer: Hi there!
> You: How are you?
> Customer: I'm good, how about you?
> You: I'm good, thanks!
> …(service happens)…
> Customer: Bye!
> You: Have a good day! See you next time!

**Pronunciation notes**

- "thanks" 的 th：舌尖轻触上齿，不是 /s/ 也不是 /f/
- "Welcome" 重音在前：WEL-come，不要读成 wel-COME

## Unit 2: 数字与价格 Numbers & Prices

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | It's thirty dollars. | 一共三十美元。 | it's THIRty dollars. |
| 2 | That's fifty in total. | 一共五十元。 | that's FIFty in total. |
| 3 | Cash or card? | 付现金还是刷卡？ | CASH or CARD? |
| 4 | Here's your change. | 这是找您的零钱。 | here's your CHANGE. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| How much is it? | 多少钱？ |
| Do you take card? | 你们可以刷卡吗？ |
| Keep the change! | 零钱不用找了！ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer walks in.
>
> You: Hi! Welcome!
> Customer: Hi there!
> …(service happens)…
> Customer: How much is it?
> You: That's fifty in total. Cash or card?
> …(customer pays in cash)…
> You: Here's your change.
> Customer: Keep the change!
> You: Have a good day! See you next time!
> Customer: Bye!

**Pronunciation notes**

- "thirty" vs "thirteen"：重音位置不同——thirty 是 THIRty（重音在前，短促收尾），thirteen 是 thirTEEN（重音在后，拖长音）。报价时读错重音，客户可能听成完全不同的数字
- "dollars" 结尾的 s：读得轻软一点，带点"嗡"的感觉，不要读成很重的"丝"音，不然会显得生硬

## Unit 3: 时间与预约用语 Time Words

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | It takes about one hour. | 大概需要一小时。 | it takes about ONE hour. |
| 2 | We open at ten. | 我们十点开门。 | we open at TEN. |
| 3 | We close at eight. | 我们八点关门。 | we close at EIGHT. |
| 4 | Today or tomorrow? | 今天还是明天？ | toDAY or toMORrow? |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| How long does it take? | 需要多长时间？ |
| What time do you close? | 你们几点关门？ |
| Are you open on Sunday? | 周日营业吗？ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer calls ahead to ask about the salon.
>
> Customer: How long does it take?
> You: It takes about one hour.
> Customer: What time do you close?
> You: We close at eight.
> Customer: Are you open on Sunday?
> You: We open at ten. Today or tomorrow?
> …(customer books an appointment)…
> Customer: Bye!
> You: Have a good day! See you next time!

**Pronunciation notes**

- "hour" 开头的 h：完全不发音，整个词读起来就是 our，不要带出气音
- "eight" 结尾的 t：轻轻收一下嘴型，别整个吞掉，不然客户可能听不出你说的是"八点"
- "today" / "tomorrow" 的重音：前面的 to 要又轻又快，重音在后半部分——toDAY、toMORrow，不要把 to 读重

## Unit 4: 自我介绍与寒暄 Small Talk

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | My name is … | 我叫……（说出自己的名字） | my NAME is … |
| 2 | Nice to meet you! | 很高兴认识你！ | nice to MEET you! |
| 3 | First time here? | 第一次来吗？ | FIRST time here? |
| 4 | Where are you from? | 你从哪里来？ | where are you FROM? |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I'm Sarah. | 我是 Sarah。 |
| Nice place! | 这地方真不错！ |
| I'm from Canada. | 我从加拿大来的。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer walks in.
>
> Customer: Hi there! Nice place!
> You: Hi! Welcome! First time here?
> Customer: I'm Sarah.
> You: My name is … Nice to meet you! Where are you from?
> Customer: I'm from Canada.
> …(service happens)…
> Customer: Bye!
> You: Have a good day! See you next time!

**Pronunciation notes**

- "meet you" 的连读：t 和 y 自然融合，听起来像 meet-choo，不用一个字一个字分开读
- "where" / "are" 里的 r：要卷一下舌头，跟中文拼音的 r 不一样，不要读成平平的"喔"
