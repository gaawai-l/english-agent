# 01 · Reception 接待与预约

## Unit 1: 进店欢迎 Welcoming

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome in! | 欢迎光临！ | WELcome in! |
| 2 | Do you have an appointment? | 你有预约吗？ | do you have an apPOINTment? |
| 3 | This way, please. | 这边请。 | this WAY, / please. |
| 4 | I'll be right with you. | 我马上就来。 | i'll be RIGHT with you. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I have an appointment at three. | 我预约了三点。 |
| It's under Sarah. | 是用 Sarah 的名字预约的。 |
| No, I don't. | 不，我没有预约。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer walks in.
>
> You: Welcome in!
> Customer: Hi there!
> You: How are you?
> Customer: I'm good, how about you?
> You: I'm good, thanks! Do you have an appointment?
> Customer: I have an appointment at three. It's under Sarah.
> You: This way, please. I'll be right with you.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "Welcome" 开头的 w：嘴唇要撅圆，像吹口哨那样吹出气流，不要咬着下嘴唇读成 v
- "appointment" 重音在中间：apPOINTment，不是开头也不是结尾，前后的音节都要读得又轻又快
- "with" 里的 th：舌尖轻轻碰一下上齿再松开，和 "thanks" 摸的位置一样，不是 s 音，也不是 f 或 v 音

## Unit 2: 问需求与约时间 Booking

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What would you like today? | 你今天想做什么？ | what would you LIKE today? |
| 2 | Which day works for you? | 你哪天方便？ | which DAY works for you? |
| 3 | We can do three o'clock tomorrow. | 明天三点可以安排。 | we can do THREE o'clock tomorrow. |
| 4 | You're all set! | 都安排好了！ | you're all SET! |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I'd like a manicure. | 我想做美甲。 |
| Do you have anything earlier? | 有更早一点的时间吗？ |
| Tomorrow afternoon works. | 明天下午可以。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer wants to book an appointment.
>
> You: What would you like today?
> Customer: I'd like a manicure.
> You: We can do three o'clock tomorrow.
> Customer: Do you have anything earlier?
> You: Which day works for you?
> Customer: Tomorrow afternoon works.
> You: You're all set!
> Customer: Bye!
> You: Have a good day! See you next time!

**Pronunciation notes**

- "would" 里的 l 不发音：整个词读起来就是 "wood"，中间的 l 完全跳过，不用刻意发出来
- "works" 结尾的 s：轻轻带过，不要把整个词吞掉，不然听起来像 "work"
- "three" 的 th：舌尖轻触上齿再松开，和 "thanks" 摸的位置一样，是这一关新出现的 th 词

## Unit 3: Walk-in 与等位 Walk-ins

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Please have a seat. | 请坐。 | please have a SEAT. |
| 2 | The wait is about twenty minutes. | 大概要等二十分钟。 | the wait is about TWENty minutes. |
| 3 | Can I get your name? | 请问你叫什么名字？ | can i get your NAME? |
| 4 | Sorry for the wait! | 抱歉让你久等了！ | SORry for the wait! |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Do you take walk-ins? | 不预约也可以直接来做吗？ |
| How long is the wait? | 要等多久？ |
| No problem, I can wait. | 没关系，我可以等。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer walks in without an appointment.
>
> Customer: Do you take walk-ins?
> You: Welcome in! Please have a seat.
> Customer: How long is the wait?
> You: The wait is about twenty minutes.
> Customer: No problem, I can wait.
> You: Can I get your name?
> Customer: I'm Sarah.
> …(twenty minutes later)…
> You: Sorry for the wait!

**Pronunciation notes**

- "seat" 结尾的 t：要轻轻收一下嘴型，别整个吞掉，不然听起来像 "see"
- "twenty" 重音在前：TWENty，和之前学过的 thirty、fifty 是同样的规律
- "get your" 连读：口语里 get 和 your 常常连在一起读，听起来像 "getcher"，不用一个字一个字分开读

## Unit 4: 引导入座与奉茶 Seating

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Follow me, please. | 请跟我来。 | FOLlow me, / please. |
| 2 | Would you like some water or tea? | 你要喝点水还是茶？ | would you like some WAter or TEA? |
| 3 | You can put your bag here. | 你的包可以放这里。 | you can put your BAG here. |
| 4 | Make yourself comfortable. | 请随意，放轻松。 | make yourself COMfortable. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Water, please. | 水就可以，谢谢。 |
| Where's the restroom? | 洗手间在哪里？ |
| Thank you! | 谢谢！ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer is ready to be seated.
>
> You: Follow me, please.
> Customer: Where's the restroom?
> You: This way, please.
> …(customer comes back)…
> You: You can put your bag here.
> Customer: Thank you!
> You: Would you like some water or tea?
> Customer: Water, please.
> You: Make yourself comfortable.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "comfortable" 重音只在第一个音节：COMfortable，后面几个音节要连着快速带过，这是最容易读错重音的词之一
- "water" 结尾的 r：要卷一下舌头，跟中文拼音的 r 不一样，美式英语里这个音很明显，不要读得太平
- "bag" 结尾的 g：轻轻带一点声音就收尾，别读得太重太清脆，不然听起来会像 "back"
