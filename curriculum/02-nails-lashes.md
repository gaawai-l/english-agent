# 02 · Nails & Lashes 美甲与美睫

## Unit 1: 选款选色 Style & Color

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What shape would you like? | 你想要什么形状？ | what SHAPE would you like? |
| 2 | Square, round, or almond? | 方形、圆形，还是杏仁形？ | SQUARE, ROUND, or ALmond? |
| 3 | Gel or regular polish? | 光疗甲油还是普通甲油？ | GEL or REGular polish? |
| 4 | Pick a color you like. | 挑一个你喜欢的颜色。 | PICK a color you like. |
| 5 | Great choice! | 选得好！ | GREAT choice! |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I want almond shape. | 我想要杏仁形的。 |
| Do you have this color? | 你们有这个颜色吗？ |
| Gel, please. | 光疗甲油，谢谢。 |
| What's popular now? | 现在流行什么？ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer is seated for a manicure.
>
> You: What shape would you like? Square, round, or almond?
> Customer: I want almond shape.
> You: Gel or regular polish?
> Customer: Gel, please.
> You: Pick a color you like.
> Customer: Do you have this color?
> You: Great choice!
> Customer: What's popular now?

**Pronunciation notes**

- "almond" 的 l 不发音：整个词读起来接近 "AH-mund"，中间的 l 完全跳过，不用刻意读出来
- "square" 开头的 sq 要连读：s 和 kw 挨在一起读，中间不要加"额"的音，不然会变成两个音节
- "regular" 重音只在第一个音节：REGular，后面几个音节要连着快速带过，跟前面学过的 "comfortable" 是同样的规律

## Unit 2: 卸甲与预处理 Removal & Prep

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do you have gel on now? | 你现在是光疗甲吗？ | do you have GEL on now? |
| 2 | I'll remove it first. | 我先帮你卸掉。 | i'll reMOVE it first. |
| 3 | Let me soak them off. | 我泡一下帮你卸掉。 | let me SOAK them off. |
| 4 | I'll trim and file your nails. | 我帮你修剪和打磨指甲。 | i'll TRIM and file your nails. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Yes, it's gel. | 对，是光疗甲。 |
| Can you make them shorter? | 可以剪短一点吗？ |
| Just a little off, please. | 剪一点点就好，谢谢。 |

**Mini dialogue**

> Customer already has gel polish on.
>
> You: Hi! Welcome!
> Customer: Hi there!
> You: Do you have gel on now?
> Customer: Yes, it's gel.
> You: I'll remove it first.
> …(files off the top layer)…
> You: Let me soak them off.
> …(nails soak)…
> You: I'll trim and file your nails.
> Customer: Can you make them shorter? Just a little off, please.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "remove" 重音在第二个音节：reMOVE，前面的 re 要读得又轻又快
- "soak" 的 oa 发长音：嘴型要拢圆一点，不要读成短促的"喔"
- "shorter" 结尾的 -er：要卷一下舌头，跟前面学过的 "water"、"comfortable" 里的 r 是同样的规律

## Unit 3: 做甲过程关怀 During the Service

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Relax your hand, please. | 手放松一下，谢谢。 | reLAX your hand, / please. |

| 3 | Am I hurting you? | 会弄疼你吗？ | am i HURTing you? |
| 4 | Almost done! | 快好了！ | almost DONE! |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|

**Mini dialogue**

> Filing and shaping the nails.
>
> You: Relax your hand, please. Is this length okay?
> Customer: A little shorter, please.
> You: Am I hurting you?
> Customer: Ouch, that one hurt a bit.
> You: Almost done!
> Customer: That's perfect.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "relax" 重音在第二个音节：reLAX，前面的 re 要读得又轻又快
- "length" 结尾的辅音连在一起：ngth 里的 th 别漏掉，舌尖要轻触一下上齿再收尾
- "hurt" 里的 ur：卷舌音，跟中文拼音的 "er" 不太一样，舌头要往后卷一点

## Unit 4: 修型与灯 Shaping & the Lamp

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do you like the shape? | 你喜欢这个形状吗？ | do you LIKE the shape? |
| 2 | I can fix this one. | 这个我可以帮你修一下。 | i can FIX this one. |
| 3 | Hand under the light, please. | 请把手放到灯下。 | hand under the LIGHT, / please. |
| 4 | Two more minutes under the lamp. | 再照两分钟灯就好。 | TWO more minutes under the lamp. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Can you make this one rounder? | 这个可以修圆一点吗？ |
| It feels a bit thick. | 感觉有点厚。 |
| How long under the lamp? | 要照灯多久？ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Checking the shape before curing.
>
> You: Do you like the shape?
> Customer: Can you make this one rounder?
> You: I can fix this one.
> …(fixes the nail)…
> Customer: It feels a bit thick.
> You: Hand under the light, please.
> Customer: How long under the lamp?
> You: Two more minutes under the lamp.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "light" 的 igh 发长音：读起来像中文的"来"，不要读得太短促
- "thick" 的 th：舌尖轻触上齿再松开，是清音，跟 "thanks" 摸的位置一样，是这一关新出现的 th 词
- "lamp" 结尾的 mp：双唇要完全闭合收尾，不要读成拖长的鼻音

## Unit 5: 美睫咨询与嫁接 Lash Consult

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Classic, hybrid, or volume? | 单根、混合，还是浓密款？ | CLASsic, HYbrid, or VOLume? |
| 2 | How full do you want them? | 你想要多浓密？ | how FULL do you want them? |
| 3 | Please close your eyes. | 请闭上眼睛。 | please CLOSE your eyes. |
| 4 | Don't get them wet for twenty-four hours. | 二十四小时内不要碰水。 | don't get them wet for TWENty-four hours. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| What's the difference? | 有什么区别？ |
| I want a natural look. | 我想要自然一点的效果。 |
| How long do they last? | 能维持多久？ |

**Mini dialogue**

> A different customer is here for a lash consult.
>
> You: Classic, hybrid, or volume?
> Customer: What's the difference?
> You: How full do you want them?
> Customer: I want a natural look.
> You: Please close your eyes.
> …(lashes are applied)…
> Customer: How long do they last?
> You: Don't get them wet for twenty-four hours.

**Pronunciation notes**

- "close" 这里是动词，结尾发浊音 z，像"克楼-z"，不要读成清音 s——跟形容词 close（靠近的）发音不一样
- "hybrid" 和 "volume" 重音都在第一个音节：HYbrid、VOLume，后面音节要轻快带过
- "twenty-four" 是复合数字：重音放在 twenty 上，跟前面学过的数字重音规律一样，four 轻快带过就好

## Unit 6: 收尾结账约下次 Checkout & Rebooking

**Core sentences**

| # | English | 中文 | 重音分块 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | All done! What do you think? | 做好了！你觉得怎么样？ | all DONE! / what do you THINK? |

| 4 | They usually last three weeks. | 一般能维持三周。 | they usually last THREE weeks. |

**Customer lines**（听懂就行，不要求会说）

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I love them! | 我很喜欢！ |
| Same time in two weeks? | 两周后同一时间？ |
| Can I get your card? | 可以给我一张你的名片吗？ |

**Mini dialogue**

> Wrapping up and checking out.
>
> You: All done! What do you think?
> Customer: I love them!
> You: That's sixty-five in total.
> …(customer pays)…
> You: Would you like to book your next visit? They usually last three weeks.
> Customer: Same time in two weeks?
> You: Great choice!
> Customer: Can I get your card?

**Pronunciation notes**

- "sixty-five" 重音在 sixty 上：SIXty-five，跟前面学过的价格数字重音规律一样
- "usually" 容易读错：中间连着读得又轻又快，像 "YOOZH-uh-lee"，不要一个音节一个音节分开读
- "visit" 重音在第一个音节：VIsit，后面的 sit 要轻快带过
